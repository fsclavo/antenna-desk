## Findings closed

1. **CI was red on `main` and `v0.6.0` even though the release workflow passed.**
   The `application` coverage gate was 89% against a 91% requirement because the
   portable-bundle import/export validation was under-tested. Added 40 tests for
   export limits, malformed manifests, unsafe ZIP entries, symlinks, encryption,
   duplicate entries, checksum/size mismatches, project validation, and
   destination cleanup. Application coverage is now 92%.
2. **Windows 3.12 timed out in CI.** The main-window test step used
   `timeout 120s`; Windows `uv` plus PySide6 startup took about 47 s before the
   ~95 s test run. Raised the step timeout to 300 s.
3. **The scheduled mutation workflow failed despite being report-only.**
   Added `continue-on-error: true` to the mutmut run step so a survivor report
   cannot mark the job red.
4. **Stale documentation:** the bundle manifest example still used
   `application_version: 0.5.0`, the roadmap still said UX24 was not part of a
   published release, and the published-version list omitted `v0.6.0`. All three
   are corrected.
5. **Lint guard tightened:** enabled the `PIE` rules, fixed the unnecessary
   `range(0, n)` starts and the duplicated `startswith` call, and kept all
   existing `noqa` directives that are still needed by the full rule set.

## Residual debt

- `PERF401` reports 20 manual list-building loops. They are micro-optimizations;
  the loops are deliberately kept where a comprehension would reduce clarity.
- G27 (frequency-dependent coax, measured transformer, common-mode choke) remains
  blocked until measurements or traceable datasets exist.
- Physical antenna validation remains blocked without measurements.
- Live VNA control, additional full-wave engines, segmented NEC frequency plans,
  and GPG/Authenticode signing remain deferred for hardware, benchmark, engine,
  or credential decisions.
- Offscreen visual QA remains necessary but is not a substitute for real-display
  theme, scaling, and font-size checks.
