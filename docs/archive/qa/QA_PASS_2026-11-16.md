## Finding and fix

The LPDA appeared in the design panel as **“Connection not classified”** and
**“unbalanced”**, even though it is a balanced center-fed beam with a
transposed phase line. The feed connection and balance classifiers did not know
the new family.

Fixed:

- `feed_balance()` treats `LogPeriodicParameters` as balanced.
- `feed_connection_detail()` classifies the LPDA as a center-fed dipole with a
  transposed phase-line note.
- The optional `feed_transformer_ratio` is reported as an ideal transformer
  when it is not 1:1.
- The design panel now recommends a 1:1 current balun/choke for a direct coax
  feed and shows the transformer interface when a ratio is configured.

Tests cover the direct, current-balun, and transformer cases.

The font-scale captures also found that `scaled_dialog_size()` could return a
dialog taller than the available screen: the LPDA editor requested 960×1350 at
1.5× and 1280×1800 at 2.0×. The helper now caps the initial size to 90 % of the
primary screen's available geometry. The content remains inside the scroll area
and the dialog stays resizable. This is a generic dialog fix, not an LPDA-only
change.

## P7/D4 decision

The P7/D4 benchmark was re-run with the new density options in
`scripts/benchmark_multiband.py`. The representative dense case uses 101
impedance/SWR points per band and five pattern frequencies per band at 5°.

- G5RV, 8 bands: 3.18 s sequential, 1.18 s with four workers, 7.05 MB.
- Fan dipole, 8 bands: 1.80 s sequential, 1.15 s with four workers, 8.53 MB.
- Solver-only, 808 frequencies: 2.43 s as eight per-band decks vs 1.48 s as one
  uniform deck.
- Per-band progress updates every 0.13–0.18 s at this density.

The per-band architecture and bounded parallel workers are retained. A
segmented multi-`FR` deck and incremental per-frequency parsing are not
justified by the measured benefit. See
[`ADR-0042`](../../ADR/0042-per-band-frequency-plan.md) and
[`MULTIBAND_PERFORMANCE.md`](../../MULTIBAND_PERFORMANCE.md).
