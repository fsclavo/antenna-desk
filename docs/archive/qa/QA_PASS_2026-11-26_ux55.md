## Result

All 25 family generator dialogs now use `FormDialogFrame`. The four compact
families keep a single column and open without a form scroll at Medium and
Large fonts. The layout guard covers every registry entry in English and
Spanish and has no exceptions.

The cross-family offscreen matrix ran **400 checks** with **0 failures**:
25 dialogs × 2 themes (light/dark) × 2 font presets (Medium/Large) × 2
languages (English/Spanish) × 2 target sizes (1366×768 and 1920×1080).

Each check required:

- the dialog renders a non-null frame;
- the OK/Cancel box is visible and inside the dialog rectangle;
- no scroll area needs a horizontal scrollbar.

Large-font dialogs were measured at their font-scaled default width, which is
how they open in the application. This matters for end-fed: at 620 px with
Large Spanish the form needs a horizontal bar, while its actual Large-font
width opens wider and does not.
