# Flower Pot FP1 closure and visual QA — 2026-11-27

- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Scope:** Flower Pot half-wave family, schema 73 feed/choke correction and
  the 3D pattern overlay anchor
- **Baseline commit:** `4d50532` (overlay anchored at the physical feed)
- **Status:** complete; automated gate green and manual visual confirmation
  received

## Result

The Flower Pot now separates the physical construction from the NEC
equivalent:

- the physical feed connector is at the bottom of the choke;
- the construction-only choke height is 45 mm on the 2 m row and 63 mm on 6 m;
- the lower cut starts at feed plus choke;
- the two-wire NEC equivalent source remains at the arm junction;
- the pattern overlay is anchored at the physical feed, so the radiating arms
  draw above the feed instead of extending below it.

The user confirmed the corrected behavior on 2026-11-27 after checking the
2 m and 6 m designs.

## Manual confirmation

The visual pass covered:

- feed marker at the base, below the choke;
- choke dimension of 45 mm (2 m) and 63 mm (6 m);
- lower cut, junction/NEC source, and electrical tail dimensions;
- **Show antenna** in the 3D pattern drawing the antenna above the physical
  feed with no downward overlay;
- construction report rows and the wire-schedule NEC-source labeling;
- the integrated-choke feed guidance with no separate current balun;
- normal desktop interaction and the existing light/dark and font-scaling
  presets.

No blocking visual or behavioral issue was found.

## Remaining limitations

- The choke remains ideal: impedance, self-resonance, common-mode current, and
  power handling are not modeled.
- The solver still uses the two-wire center-fed equivalent at the arm junction;
  it does not model the coax inner/outer shield currents or the choke as a
  circuit element.
- No 50 Ω, gain, bandwidth, or validated choke claim is made.
- The 5/8, double 5/8, dual-band sleeve, and high-gain variants remain out of
  scope until their own spikes and ADRs exist.
